# Cuelinks web app: hands-on gap review, 2026-10-26

What I tried: 17 public pages on desktop (1366 px) and mobile (Pixel 7), the campaign directory (search, category/country filters, sort, paging), campaign detail pages, login and forgot-password edge cases, signup validation, the mobile menu, and every nav/footer/tools link. Logged-in pages could not be reviewed (see #1). All tests used the QA Test5064 account from `REPORT-2026-10-26.md` or no account; no new accounts were created.

Severity: **P1** breaks a core task or blocks users · **P2** misleads users or loses conversions · **P3** polish.

## P1: broken or blocking

### 1. Cloudflare challenge on the signed-in area *and* on Contact Us
- Every page after login redirects to `/users/edit` (the "verify your channel" step), and that page shows Cloudflare's "Verify you are human" challenge. With a session, even public pages like `/campaigns/flipkart-affiliate-program` redirect there first.
- **`/contact-us` is challenged even when logged out.** It's the page behind the "Contact Sales" CTA on the homepage and the "Contact Us" link on the 404 page. In headless Chromium it never loaded (60 s timeout, 403).
- It's possible only automated browsers see this. But the rule clearly targets particular paths (other public pages load cleanly), so it's worth checking Cloudflare security events for which rule fires on `/contact-us` and `/users/*` (rays `a508cbc9d9d69acd`, `a508d8047ec4a177`, `a508e14ceeabdda7`). If real prospects or advertisers see it, the sales funnel loses people at its last step.

### 2. Category filter: clicking a category name does nothing
- In the "Select Category" dropdown on `/campaigns`, each row's label is `<label for="category-4">`, but the checkbox it belongs to has `id="4"`. The label isn't connected, so clicking "Electronics" (the large target) does nothing, and Apply then sends `/campaigns?` with no filter. Only the 16 px checkbox itself works. On mobile that's a very small tap target.
- I confirmed this both ways. Clicking the label changed nothing. Ticking the checkbox sent `?category_ids[]=4` and the list filtered correctly.
- Fix: make the ids match (`id="category-4"`). The country list probably has the same pattern and should be checked.

### 3. "3 Months EPC – High to Low" sorts low to high (and vice versa)
- The sort options in the page source:
  - `3 Months EPC - High to Low` → `['epc_for_90_days', 'asc']`
  - `3 Months EPC - Low to High` → `['epc_for_90_days', 'desc']`
- Choosing "High to Low" puts ₹0.0-EPC campaigns at the top, which is the opposite of what a publisher looking for the best campaigns wants.
- Related: **"7 Top Converting"** and **"7 Most Clicked"** use `cr_for_30_days` and `clicks_for_30_days`. The labels look like a missing "Days" *and* the wrong window. They should be "30 Days Top Converting" or use 7-day data.

### 4. Signup: the website help tooltip covers the Sign Up button
- While the "Website or Social Media URL" field has focus, its tooltip (`#website-tooltip`, y 679–733 px) overlaps the Sign Up button (y 707–749 px). The first click lands on the tooltip and nothing happens. Since the website field is the last one, almost everyone has it focused when they go to click Sign Up.
- Fix: hide the tooltip when focus leaves the field, give it `pointer-events: none`, or put it above the field.

## P2: misleading or costs conversions

5. **Search by payout type doesn't work.** Both the placeholder ("Search for Flipkart or Electronics or CPI etc") and the intro copy ("search using … payout types (CPS, CPI etc.)") promise it. Searching "CPI" actually matches text inside merchant names, returning *Authenti**cpi**zzaovens* and *Mo**cpi**xel* alongside the one real app-install campaign. Either support type search, or change the copy and add a Type filter.
6. **Flipkart payout disagrees across pages.** The directory row says "Upto 7.20% Per Sale", but the detail page title and SEO title say "Upto 12% Per Sale".
7. **Campaign counts disagree across sites.** www.cuelinks.com says "1000+ campaigns" everywhere, while developers.cuelinks.com and mcp.cuelinks.com say "10,000+ campaigns", "20,000+ brands" and "100+ networks". Prospects comparing pages will notice.
8. **The directory's default view is mostly foreign campaigns with ₹0.0 EPC.** Page 1 is Nykaa, then LegalZoom (US), Oppo UK, TYMO (US), Fazari (US) and so on. 12 of 15 rows show "₹ 0.0" 3-month EPC. For an India-first audience, the first impression is "nothing here earns". Consider defaulting to India, or sorting by EPC (once #3 is fixed).
9. **Duplicate or confusing listings.**
   - `flipkart` vs `flipkart-1`: the second is "Flipkart Seller Kids" (a CPL seller-signup campaign). Its breadcrumb reads "Flipkart Affiliate Program" and its SEO title is "Flipkart Affiliate Program with Payout ₹ 855.00 / Sale". It has no logo, and its "Similar Campaigns" are unrelated foreign merchants (Raketacn, Swaz, Printvoordeelshop) with "Payout: Upto 100" and no unit.
   - Other slugs with numeric suffixes (`lenovo-1`, `philips-2`) suggest more duplicates.
   - The category list has both **"Food & Grocery"** and **"Grocery & Food"**. "Installs" sits next to "Mobiles" and "Software", and it's a payout type rather than a category.
10. **Six pages load a deleted animation.** `/why-cuelinks`, `/about-us`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/login` and `/thank-you` each request a lottie.host JSON file that returns 403. Each file is different, but all are gone. On `/thank-you` this leaves a visibly empty card (see the earlier report). All pages also load `@dotlottie/player-component@latest` from unpkg without a pinned version.
11. **Paging isn't linkable.** Next/Previous fetch `?page=N` via AJAX, but the address bar stays `/campaigns`. Refresh or Back returns to page 1, and a filtered or sorted view can't be shared.
12. **Country tags are cut off** in the directory ("United S", "United K", "Hong Kon", "Australi"). The country filter lists all 251 ISO countries, including Antarctica, Aland Islands and American Samoa, with no indication of which have any campaigns.

## P3: polish

13. **Copy:**
    - The LetsEarn banner on home and campaign pages says "Earnings Starts with a Tap" (should be "Earnings Start…").
    - The mobile menu says "Chrome Extention".
    - The homepage says "Over 60,000+ Publishers" and "Over 1000+ Advertisers" ("Over" and "+" say the same thing).
    - The email footer still links Google+.
14. **Stale footer:** "© Copyright 2024 Parity Cube Private Limited." appears on every page except the blog (2026), MCP (2026) and developers (2026) sites.
15. **SEO basics:**
    - `/blog/` has no meta description, a title ending in a stray " -", and 11 of 13 images without alt text.
    - The homepage has 11 of 38 images without alt text.
    - The 404 page has an empty `<title>`.
    - Most detail-page titles end "| October 2026" but Flipkart's doesn't.
    - `mcp.cuelinks.com` has no canonical tag.
16. **About Us mixes in job ads** ("Minimum 4+ Years of experience…"). Company, Team and Careers in the footer all go to the same `/about-us` page.
17. **Signup validation is mostly HTML5-only.** A website value of "notaurl" is caught, but `x@y` passes as an email and password "123" isn't flagged on the page. I couldn't check what the server does without a valid phone. The example.com rule (see earlier report) only shows up after a server round trip.

## What worked well
- **Login and forgot password:** both give the same message for known and unknown emails, so they don't reveal which accounts exist ("Invalid email or password" / "You will receive an email…"). The reset email arrived within a minute.
- **Signup → confirm → login** works end to end (see `REPORT-2026-10-26.md`).
- **Mobile layout:** no horizontal overflow on any of the 17 pages. The menu opens and exposes all sections.
- **Links:** every internal nav, footer and tools link resolves (200). The only exceptions are `/contact-us` (#1) and the deleted lottie files (#10).
- **Search basics:** search-as-you-type autocomplete is fast and relevant for merchant names. The empty state ("Campaign Not Found") exists.

## Not covered
- Channel verification, dashboard, link generation, reports, payouts and referral tracking for logged-in users. These are blocked by #1 and need either a Cloudflare skip rule for the QA runner or a manual session.
- Facebook / Google sign-in.
- Server-side validation of signup fields (each check would need a real submission with the test phone).

## Suggested order
1. #2, #3 and #4: small front-end fixes with a direct effect on core tasks.
2. #1: check the Cloudflare rules on `/contact-us` and `/users/*`.
3. #6, #7 and #9: data and copy consistency.
4. The rest as polish.
